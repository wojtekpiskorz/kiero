## Result and candidate rules

This register records partial evidence and current owners. It does not mark an entire proof group PASS based on a merged PR. Every assigned case remains NOT RUN, BLOCKED, FAIL or PASS until a dated artifact supplies its current result. Case-level rows live under the owning issue's evidence directory; J5 assembles them without losing failed runs.

J3 uses the accepted threshold of at least 48/50 in each of three runs with zero critical errors. Disclosed non-critical corpus failures within that threshold do not become fictional PASS rows. Mandatory operational and failure-handling proofs must pass.

J6 records the candidate commit, lockfile hash, component digests, migrations, provider/model/prompt/schema versions, runtime bindings, fixture hashes and declared expectations. Later changes trigger an explicit impact check. Rerun affected cases on the final candidate; carry unaffected evidence only with a recorded justification based on unchanged relevant code/configuration. Preserve J4 elapsed Google authorization evidence only when its account, OAuth and integration basis remain applicable. A new qualifying interval is required if that basis changes.

## External resources and closure

I8 owns authorized staging topology and binding verification. I6 owns quarantine isolation. The [owner resource instruction](https://github.com/wojtekpiskorz/kiero/issues/15#issuecomment-5618841487) requires refs under existing `kiero-dev-core`; no project creation may bypass limits. B5/J4 require two real Google accounts and ordinary email authentication. D7 requires actual retained media executors. J4 requires physical devices and more than seven elapsed days. I11 requires an approved owner-alert destination and actual delivery; synthetic alert conditions are permitted, simulated delivery is not. J5 joins actual I10 backup/provider cost measurements.

Unavailable mandatory resources or elapsed time keep the owning issue OPEN/BLOCKED. Record the responsible actor, exact missing action and resumption trigger. Successful code integration does not close that proof obligation.

J5 is the final assembler and acceptance owner for every P01-P12 row. Provider/model and cost fields may be null when inapplicable; record a concrete notApplicableReason instead of inventing a value.
