## Cron audit (pushed bundle, dev/main)

`convex/crons.ts` runs eight safety nets: two at 1-minute cadence
(telemetry tick, attention-intent evaluator), five at 5-minute
(outbox drain, calendar sync, reminders, push, plus the
attention-push net) and two at 15-minute (backup schedule tick,
deletion purge tick) — roughly 4.2k cron invocations/day with zero
users (~13% of the monthly function-call allowance, before any real
work). The cadences are deliberate lane decisions (F2/F4/I5/I6
"quota guardrail" notes in the file); trimming them for the pre-user
phase is an owner/lane decision under the ADR's waste-first rule, not
something this session did unilaterally. The usage-limits above bound
the blast radius in the meantime.
